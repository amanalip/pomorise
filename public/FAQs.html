Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about focusing, tasks, privacy, browser permissions, local storage, and keeping your Pomorise data safe.

What is Pomorise?

Pomorise is a calm Pomodoro-style focus timer. It helps you choose one task, work in a timed session, take a break, and record a short private reflection. It works without an account and keeps your information in your browser.

How do focus and break sessions work?

Focus, short-break, and long-break modes have separate durations. The defaults are 25, 5, and 15 minutes. You can change each duration in Settings. Starting, pausing, adding a minute, skipping, resetting, and continuing in overtime are all explicit controls.

What does “Session 1 of 3” mean?

When a task is selected, this shows your current session within that task's estimate. For example, “Session 1 of 3” means the task was estimated at three focus sessions and no session has yet been carried forward. Without a selected task, the number describes Pomorise's regular four-session focus cycle.

How do tasks work?

Add a short task, estimate it at one to eight focus sessions, and choose which unfinished task is current. Pomorise allows up to five unfinished tasks at a time so the list stays manageable. Completed tasks remain available in Task history and do not consume those five spaces.

What is the difference between completing and carrying a task forward?

Mark complete when the work itself is finished. Carry forward records one completed focus session against the current task and keeps that task selected for another session. The task history shows completed sessions beside the original estimate.

Where can I review previous tasks?

Open Tasks and expand Task history. It lists completed tasks with their completed and estimated focus-session totals. This history stays in the same browser unless you delete all focus data or replace it by importing a backup.

What does Progress show?

Progress summarizes completed focus sessions and focus minutes for today, plus sessions completed during the last seven days. These numbers are calculated from records stored privately in this browser.

What is Quick capture?

Quick capture lets you save a distracting thought without stopping the timer. After the focus session, you can turn it into a task, keep it for later, or dismiss it.

Are reflections required?

No. A reflection can include a next step, focus rating, and private notes, but every part is optional and the complete reflection can be skipped.

Where is my data stored?

Tasks, sessions, captured thoughts, and reflections are stored in this browser profile using IndexedDB. Timer and appearance preferences use local browser storage. Pomorise has no account system, application database, analytics, advertising tracker, or cloud sync.

What does Storage protection do?

Browsers can remove site data when device storage is low. Storage protection asks the browser to reduce that risk. The browser may decline, so it is still important to export a backup. Private-browsing data remains temporary even when this feature is available.

How do backup and restore work?

Download backup creates a JSON file locally containing your structured Pomorise data. Export sessions CSV creates a spreadsheet-friendly session history. Import previews a selected backup before it replaces the current tasks and history. Nothing is uploaded by Pomorise during these actions.

Why did Chrome not show a notification prompt?

Chrome normally asks the first time you enable notifications. If you previously blocked or dismissed the request, Chrome may stop showing it. Select the site-controls icon beside the address bar, open the site's permissions, allow Notifications, and then try the Pomorise setting again.

Why is there no Chrome permission prompt for sound?

Chrome does not provide websites with a sound permission-request prompt like the notification prompt. Pomorise asks for confirmation before enabling its locally generated completion tone. If the tone remains silent, use Chrome's site controls to allow Sound and confirm that the tab and device are not muted.

Can Pomorise work offline?

Yes. After one complete online visit, supported browsers can reopen the installed or cached application offline. Updates wait for your approval and never replace an active timer automatically.

How do I delete my information?

Open Settings, then Data & privacy. Clear history removes sessions, reflections, and captured thoughts while keeping tasks and preferences. Delete everything removes tasks and history. Reset preferences changes only appearance and timer choices.

Does Pomorise work across devices?

There is no cloud synchronization. Each browser profile has its own local data. To move information between devices, download a backup on one device and import it on the other.

Where can I report a problem or inspect the code?

Pomorise is developed in a public GitHub repository. You can inspect the source or report an issue at github.com/amanalip/pomorise.
